The purpose of the dashboard is to show how the IBM team will transition from a traditional managed-services / steady-state delivery mindset into a transformation-led operating ecosystem.

This is not just a project tracker. It should visually show the team’s journey into new ways of working, new behaviors, new governance rhythms, new tooling adoption, and measurable transformation outcomes.

Use React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion, Lucide icons, and Recharts.

Keep the functionality simple but make the design fancy, polished, and client-demo ready.

Core Narrative

IBM will help Horizon evolve from steady-state operations into a transformation ecosystem by shifting the team mindset across:

From ticket execution to outcome ownership
From reactive support to proactive improvement
From project delivery to product-based thinking
From manual operations to AIOps and automation
From inconsistent Agile usage to disciplined Agile ways of working
From static reporting to CareOps360 governance visibility
From activity tracking to value realization
From vendor dependency to IBM-led Quality Engineering transition

Quality Engineering transition should appear last as an enabling workstream, not the lead story.

7. Risks and Mitigations Panel

Show a compact premium panel for risks and mitigations.

Include these risks:

Risk: Team may continue to operate in task-execution mode.
Mitigation: Reinforce outcome-based KPIs, governance reviews, and ownership-based accountability.
Risk: Agile adoption may remain superficial.
Mitigation: Use practical coaching, backlog quality checks, sprint health metrics, and team-level adoption reviews.
Risk: Automation may remain secondary to daily operations.
Mitigation: Establish automation intake, prioritization, and weekly review of repeatable manual work.
Risk: CareOps360 may become a reporting tool instead of a governance behavior.
Mitigation: Tie CareOps360 usage to weekly leadership reviews and decision tracking.
Risk: Product model may be defined but not adopted.
Mitigation: Align roles, ownership, metrics, and decision rights to product/platform teams.
Risk: QE transition may remain knowledge-transfer focused without mindset shift.
Mitigation: Use shadow/reverse-shadow, quality ownership metrics, automation assessment, and IBM readiness sign-off.
