Build a **“Digital Roadmap”** section for the **CareOps360** platform using a simple, intuitive, executive-friendly UI.

## Objective

Provide a clean view of **upgrades, ticket reduction initiatives, and transformation activities** across portfolios, with the ability to track timeline, status, priority, and expected outcomes.

## Professional Disclaimer

Include this note clearly on the page, in small professional text below the page title or in the footer:

**Disclaimer:** This dashboard contains representative initiative data derived from planning materials for demonstration and prototype purposes only. It should not be interpreted as a final approved delivery plan, production commitment, or live operational record.

---

## UI Layout

Create a simple 3-part layout:

### 1. Top Section: Summary Cards

Show:

* Total Initiatives
* Active Initiatives
* Planned Initiatives
* Completed Initiatives
* High Priority Initiatives

### 2. Middle Section: Roadmap Timeline

* Show initiatives by quarter across CY1
* Each initiative appears as a horizontal bar
* Color-code by initiative type:

  * Upgrade
  * Ticket Reduction
  * Transformation
* Hover shows quick details
* Click opens the initiative detail panel

### 3. Bottom Section: Initiatives Table

Display all initiatives in a clean table
