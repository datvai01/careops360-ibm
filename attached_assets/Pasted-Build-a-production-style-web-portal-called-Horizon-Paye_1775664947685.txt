Build a production-style web portal called Horizon Payer Command Center for IBM to showcase Application Management Services value to Horizon BCBS leadership.

The portal should be a modern executive dashboard with a clean enterprise healthcare design, optimized for desktop, tablet, and large screens. It should simulate a real-time “single pane of glass” view across payer operations, business KPIs, and application health.

Objective

Create a unified platform that gives business and IT leadership a live operational view of:

Member Enrollment
Claims Intake and Settlements
Prior Authorization
Contact Center / Member Service Operations
Provider Operations
Billing / Premium / Payments
Care Management / Utilization trends
Application Health, incidents, batch jobs, interfaces, and SLA compliance
IBM AMS service performance and transformation value
Users

Create role-based views for:

Executive leadership
Operations leadership
IT leadership / CIO dashboard
Application support managers
IBM delivery leadership
