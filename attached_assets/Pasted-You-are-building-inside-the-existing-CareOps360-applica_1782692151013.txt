Business pain:

* High operational cost from legacy batch processing and platform complexity.
* Online screens unavailable during long batch windows.
* File-based processing creates troubleshooting delays.
* Limited end-to-end balancing and claim/payment tracking visibility.
* Heavy document and print dependency.
* Limited agility for new payment methods, reporting needs, and operational controls.

* Average daily claims processed: 500K
* Average daily payment amount: $90M

* Average daily printed pages: 60K
* Reports generated: 150+
* Batch execution window: ~10 hours
* Concurrent users: 50
* Online screens: 130+
* Batch jobs: ~300
* Code modules: ~480
* Large legacy codebase: ~1M lines

Target outcomes:

* Reduce batch window from ~10 hours to 2–4 hours initially, then progressively move to event-driven processing.
* Improve business availability toward 24/7 access.
* Reduce printed output by 40% – 70%.
* Improve payment traceability and reconciliation.
* Reduce operational issue resolution time by 30% – 50%.
* Enable cloud-native scalability on AWS.
* Improve compliance, auditability, and operational controls.

Directional annual value:
$8M – $14M

Value levers:

* Print and document reduction
* Reduced payment operations rework
* Faster payment exception resolution
* Improved provider and subscriber experience
* Risk reduction from better reconciliation and audit trail

* Even a happy path can take around 48 hours to reach downstream availability.
* A single failure can add 24 hours.
* Multiple cascading failures can stretch processing to 5 days.
* There is limited observability across intake, validation, mastering, and downstream publication.
* Delays impact newborn coverage, prior authorization readiness, member letters, call center volumes, and compliance exposure.
