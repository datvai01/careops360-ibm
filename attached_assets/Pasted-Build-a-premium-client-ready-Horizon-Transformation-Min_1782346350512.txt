Build a premium, client-ready **Horizon Transformation Mindset Survey & Dynamic Transition Plan** web app.

IBM is the incumbent operations partner. The current environment is generally stable, but Horizon wants the IBM-Horizon team to evolve from steady-state operations into a more proactive, transformation-led mindset and ecosystem.

This app will be used during a workshop. IBM should be able to share an anonymous survey link with participants. Participants will provide candid feedback, and the facilitator dashboard should dynamically aggregate the responses and generate an AI-suggested transition plan.

Use React, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui, Framer Motion, Lucide icons, Recharts, Node/Express backend, and SQLite or Replit DB.

Use OpenAI API for AI-generated recommendations using environment variable `OPENAI_API_KEY`. If the API key is unavailable, use a rule-based fallback recommendation engine.

---

## Core Positioning

The app should not imply that current operations are broken.

Use constructive language:

* Stable operations
* Next level of maturity
* Proactive improvement
* Transformation-led mindset
* Automation readiness
* Intelligent operations readiness
* Governance visibility
* Outcome ownership
* Product/platform ownership
* Value realization

Avoid negative language such as:

* Failed operations
* Broken process
* Poor performance
* Lack of capability

Do not assume AIOps is already implemented. Refer to it as future readiness for automation and intelligent operations.

Quality Engineering transition from the current vendor should be referenced as a later enabling workstream in the AI-generated transition plan, but it should not dominate the survey.

## Survey Questions — Exactly 20 Total

Create exactly 20 questions.

### Section 1: Current Operations Stability

1. How would you rate the overall stability of current operations?

2. How consistently are services delivered against expected SLAs and commitments?

3. How confident are you that current operations can support future transformation without disruption?

### Section 2: Incident and High-Priority Incident Management

4. How effectively are high-priority incidents managed from identification to resolution?

5. How clearly are roles, responsibilities, and communication handled during major incidents?

6. How well are recurring issues, root causes, and preventive actions identified and tracked?

### Section 3: Proactive Improvement Mindset

7. How ready is the team to shift from reactive support to proactive improvement?

8. How often does the team identify improvement opportunities before issues occur?

9. How effectively are lessons learned converted into sustained operational improvements?

### Section 4: Data, Automation, and Intelligent Operations Readiness

Do not assume AIOps is already implemented. Position this section as future readiness for automation and intelligent operations.

10. How effectively does the team use operational data, trends, and patterns to drive improvements?

11. How ready is the environment for increased automation and intelligent operations capabilities in the future?

12. How clearly are automation opportunities identified, prioritized, and tracked today?

### Section 5: Agile and Transformation Ways of Working

13. How consistently are Agile ways of working used where applicable, including backlog discipline, prioritization, and team ceremonies?

14. How ready are teams to adopt Agile discipline in selected transformation areas?

15. How effectively are business priorities translated into delivery actions and measurable outcomes?

### Section 6: Governance and Visibility

16. How effective is current governance visibility across operations, risks, decisions, and transformation activities?

17. How useful would a CareOps360-style governance dashboard be for weekly leadership visibility and decision-making?

### Section 7: Product / Outcome Ownership

18. How ready is the organization to move toward product/platform ownership and outcome-based accountability?

### Section 8: Open Feedback

19. What are the top 2–3 changes that would help the IBM-Horizon team move from stable operations to a transformation-led mindset?

20. What risks, concerns, or dependencies should IBM and Horizon address early to make this transformation successful?

## AI-Suggested Transition Plan

Create a dashboard section called:

**AI-Suggested Transition Plan**

When the facilitator clicks “Generate / Refresh AI Transition Plan,” analyze aggregate survey scores and open-text responses to generate:

1. Executive summary
2. Current strengths
3. Transformation opportunities
4. Recommended 30 / 60 / 90-day transition plan
5. Priority actions
6. Risks and mitigations
7. Suggested workshop discussion themes

The AI response must use this framing:

“Given that current operations are stable, the transition plan should focus on moving the IBM-Horizon team toward a transformation-led ecosystem, not correcting failed operations.”

The AI-generated plan should recommend practical actions such as:

* Strengthen outcome-based governance
* Establish transformation mindset checkpoints
* Identify automation candidates from recurring work
* Improve incident prevention and root cause discipline
* Introduce Agile discipline in selected transformation areas
* Launch CareOps360-style governance visibility
* Clarify product/platform ownership
* Define value realization metrics
* Position Quality Engineering transition as a later enabling workstream
* Use QE transition to reinforce ownership, automation, defect governance, and quality mindset

## Rule-Based Fallback Recommendation Engine

If OpenAI API is unavailable, generate recommendations using these rules.

### Operations Stability

If Operations Stability average is below 3.5:

Recommend strengthening operational confidence before scaling transformation, including SLA transparency, service dependency mapping, and operational readiness checkpoints.

### High-Priority Incident Management

If Incident Management average is below 3.5:

Recommend a major incident playbook, clearer role ownership, communication discipline, RCA quality improvement, and preventive action tracking.

### Proactive Improvement

If Proactive Improvement average is below 3.5:

Recommend recurring issue analysis, improvement backlog creation, weekly improvement reviews, and ownership for proactive service improvement.

### Automation & Intelligent Operations Readiness

If Automation Readiness average is below 3.5:

Recommend automation opportunity intake, monitoring signal review, operational data readiness, prioritization of repeatable manual work, and future AIOps readiness planning.

### Agile Ways of Working

If Agile average is below 3.5:

Recommend selected Agile pilot teams, backlog hygiene, sprint planning, reviews, retrospectives, and practical coaching without implying that Horizon must adopt Agile everywhere immediately.

### Governance Visibility

If Governance Visibility average is below 3.5:

Recommend CareOps360-style dashboard, decision log, risk tracker, weekly governance cadence, and leadership visibility into transformation progress.

### Product / Outcome Ownership

If Product Ownership average is below 3.5:

Recommend product/service taxonomy, ownership model, decision rights, outcome metrics, and alignment of teams to products/platforms rather than only projects or tasks.

### General Strength

If most dimensions are 3.5 or above:

Recommend moving directly into a transformation acceleration plan focused on automation, governance visibility, product ownership, and value realization.
