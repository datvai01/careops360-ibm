Enhance the CareOps360 platform by adding a new feature inside the Command Center module called:

“Capability Uplift & AI Readiness”

This feature should showcase how the delivery team is evolving from current capabilities to planned AI-enabled capabilities, and how this directly drives business value for Horizon.

The experience should feel like an executive-grade dashboard, not a static report.

--------------------------------------------------
📍 Placement in App
--------------------------------------------------

- Module: Command Center (CareOps360)
- Add a new tab or section:
  → “Capability Uplift”
- Also allow quick access from:
  → Member Enrollment Intelligence → link: “View Team Capability Evolution”

--------------------------------------------------
🎯 Objective
--------------------------------------------------

Provide a clear, visual, and data-driven view of:

1. Current certified capabilities
2. Planned certification and skill uplift
3. Alignment to Digital Front Door layers
4. Business value impact

This should help leadership understand:
“How IBM capability evolution is enabling Horizon transformation”
